BACKGROUND

I am pursuing a Ph.D. in Engineering at Louisiana Tech University, with a concentration in Materials, Systems, and Infrastructure, under the supervision of Dr. Md Rubel Ahmed.

Before entering graduate research, I spent over eight years in power generation at Ashuganj Power Station Company Ltd. My experience in electrical maintenance, protection, troubleshooting, and safe plant operation informs my work on resilient power systems and AI data center integration.

I hold a bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering from Khulna University of Engineering & Technology.
