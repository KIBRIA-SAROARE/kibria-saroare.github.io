About Me

I am an Electrical Engineer and Researcher focused on advancing resilient, data-driven power systems by uniting real-world utility experience with cutting-edge hardware and AI-driven grid intelligence.

I am currently a Graduate Research Assistant at Louisiana Tech University, pursuing a Ph.D. in Engineering with a concentration in Materials, Systems, and Infrastructure under the supervision of Dr. Md Rubel Ahmed. My research centers on Spatio-Temporal Grid Resilience and Hardware-Aware Signal Processing, where I design FPGA-accelerated algorithms and physics-informed digital twins to support hyperscale AI data center integration and next-generation smart grid monitoring.

Before transitioning into research, I spent over 8 years in the power generation industry, supporting a 1690 MW national utility at Ashuganj Power Station Company Ltd. As a Senior Electrical Engineer, I led high-impact reliability improvements, regulatory compliance programs, and grid stability initiatives across mission-critical infrastructure. This hands-on operational background gives me a grounded understanding of the challenges modern grids face—and the technologies required to solve them.

I completed my B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET). My work has been recognized through the NSF & Louisiana Board of Regents GRASS Grant, reflecting my commitment to impactful, application-driven research at the intersection of power systems, hardware acceleration, and intelligent grid control.
